# Streaming words: register review (not a native sign-off)

19 non-English locales x 15 words. All present, none left in English. The sets are
creative idiomatic equivalents rather than literal translations, which is the right
instinct. Flags below are register and ambiguity, from reading, not from a native ear
and not verified on device (app language cannot be changed in the simulator).

## 1. The voice changes by language. This is the systematic one.

| Form | Locales | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Progressive / gerund | es, it, pt-BR, hi, vi, th | Analizando, กำลังวิเคราะห์ |
| Aspectual (-中 / -중) | zh-CN, zh-TW, ja, ko | 分析中, 분석 중 |
| **First person "I am ..."** | ar, pl, ru, uk, tr | Myślę, Думаю, Düşünüyorum |
| **Bare infinitive** | de, nl | Denken, Analysieren |
| **Nominal** | fr | Réflexion, Analyse |

Mera introduces itself in the first person in some languages and as an impersonal
process in others. Pick one and regenerate against it. The impersonal progressive is
the only form every language here supports naturally.

## 2. Specific words worth a native eye

- **ru `Настаиваю`** (Percolating). "Настаивать" does mean to steep, but it far more
  commonly means "I insist". In a reply bubble that is a real misread. Highest-priority
  single item. uk `Настоюю` is less exposed.
- **de / nl bare infinitives.** `Denken`, `Analysieren`, `Denken`, `Analyseren` read as
  nouns or commands rather than as something in progress. German and Dutch loading copy
  normally uses a progressive or nominal-passive construction.
- **tr `Derinleşiyorum`** (Contemplating) is "I am deepening", which is not the sense.
- **fr `Mijotage`, `Macération`** are rare or technical nouns (the latter is winemaking
  and cooking vocabulary). `Ça mijote` is the idiom.
- **es `Macerando`, it `Macerando`, pt-BR `Macerando`** carry the same technical smell.
  The neighbouring `Rumiando` / `Rimuginando` / `Matutando` are good.
- **ru / uk `Кумекаю`** is colloquial and rustic. That matches "Noodling" in register,
  so this is a check rather than a flag.

## 3. Clean

ar, ja, ko, zh-CN, zh-TW, hi, th, vi, id read well and are internally consistent.
